# Photographer Mission Control Home Pulse Release Announcement

## What Changed
Photographer Mission Control now opens with a new Home Pulse experience built to show the live operational heartbeat of the business. The home screen is now centered on six high-value widgets in this order:

- Business Pulse
- Today's Shoots
- Weather and Travel Watch
- Customer Service Pulse
- Places That Need More Love
- Labor Snapshot Today, when enabled

This same model now powers the office status board and TV mode in a public-safe format.

## Why It Matters
The previous dashboard carried too much private workflow detail too early. That made the landing experience heavier than it needed to be and less useful as a fast daily command surface.

The new version is designed to help leadership and office teams answer the most important questions in seconds:

- How busy are we this week?
- What is happening today?
- What may affect the day?
- Where do we need extra operational care?
- Is support steady?

## What Is Better Now
- The home screen is more condensed and easier to scan.
- The most important business counts are visible at the top.
- Today's Shoots is now the visual centerpiece.
- Deep detail still exists, but it opens on demand instead of cluttering the landing page.
- The office status board now uses a true public-safe mode rather than simply hiding fields visually.

## What Is Intentionally Not On The Main Home Screen
To keep the home experience operational and office-safe, the main surface does not lead with:

- attendance exceptions
- staffing gaps
- approvals waiting
- payroll detail
- individual exception reasons
- sensitive personnel issues

Those workflows still exist elsewhere in Mission Control. They are no longer the first thing the whole company sees when the dashboard opens.

## TV Mode
TV mode now has its own rendering path and is designed for honest, safe office visibility.

By default it:

- hides employee names
- avoids private workflow detail
- rotates through a small set of readable operational pages
- keeps the focus on business pulse, today's shoots, weather and travel, customer service health, and warm location context

## What Comes Next
This release improves the command surface now. The next upgrades are expected to focus on:

- live production queue data for ID card visibility
- live weather and route ETA integrations
- additional TV board controls for page order and rotation timing

## Leadership Readout
Mission Control now opens with a clearer, safer, and more useful daily operating view. The business pulse is easier to read, today's work is easier to understand, and the office board is more appropriate for shared display. This is a meaningful step toward making Mission Control the daily operational screen for the company.
